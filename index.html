Introduction

Rising temperatures are a major concern under global climate change, but warming can also be influenced by local factors. Urbanization is one such factor, as the expansion of impervious surfaces such as roads, buildings, and parking lots may affect local temperatures. The five-county study area around Buffalo contains different levels of development, providing an opportunity to examine the relationship between warming and urbanization. This project will analyze temperature trends from 2001 to 2025 and investigate whether they are associated with changes in urbanization.
